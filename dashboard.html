Frequently Asked Questions

What is TOONZI Exchange?

TOONZI Exchange is a worldwide micro-task platform where users can earn points by completing small tasks from other users. It operates on a Follow4Follow model, meaning you complete tasks for others and get your tasks completed by the community. With users from 30+ countries, it's a global platform for earning rewards.

How do I earn coins on TOONZI Exchange?

You earn coins by completing tasks posted by other users. Each completed task adds coins to your account balance. The more tasks you complete, the more coins you earn. Additionally, if you are among the first 30 users from your country, you receive a 40-coin welcome bonus. Your coins can be used to post your own tasks for others to complete.

What is the Daily Task Tracker?

The Daily Task Tracker shows your progress for the day. You can complete up to 20 tasks per day. The progress bar fills up as you complete tasks, helping you track your daily activity. This feature encourages consistent participation and helps you maximize your earnings. Resetting your daily progress allows you to start fresh the next day.

How does the Follow4Follow model work?

The Follow4Follow model is simple: you complete tasks for other users, and they complete tasks for you. It's a community-driven approach where everyone helps each other. When you post a task, other users will complete it, and you earn coins when they do. Similarly, when you complete tasks for others, you earn coins. It's a mutually beneficial system that builds a strong community.

Is TOONZI Exchange free to join?

Yes, TOONZI Exchange is completely free to join. There are no hidden fees or subscription costs. Simply create an account with your email and password, and you can start earning coins immediately. The platform is designed to be accessible to everyone, regardless of their location or financial situation.

Which countries are supported on TOONZI Exchange?

TOONZI Exchange supports users from 30+ countries worldwide. The platform automatically detects your country based on your IP address when you sign up. This allows us to provide country-specific bonuses and ensure a diverse global community. Whether you're from India, the United States, the United Kingdom, or any other supported country, you can join and start earning.

How do I reset my daily task progress?

You can reset your daily task progress by clicking the "Reset Daily Progress" button on your dashboard. This resets your tasks completed today to zero, allowing you to start fresh. Resetting is useful if you want to track your progress from the beginning of the day or if you want to set new goals for yourself.

What happens if I forget my password?

If you forget your password, click the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page. Enter your registered email address, and we'll send you a password reset link. Follow the link to create a new password and regain access to your account. Your coins and task history remain safe and secure.

Is my personal information safe on TOONZI Exchange?

Yes, your personal information is completely safe on TOONZI Exchange. We use Firebase Authentication for secure login and Firestore for data storage. Your password is encrypted, and we do not share your information with third parties. The platform is designed with privacy and security as top priorities.

How can I contact support?

You can contact our support team through the Contact Us page. We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours. Whether you have questions about tasks, earnings, or technical issues, our support team is here to help. You can also reach us via email at info@toonzi.xyz.
