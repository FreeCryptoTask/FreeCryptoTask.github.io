1. Cryptocurrency Kya Hai?

Decentralization

Cryptocurrency kisi bhi bank ya government ke control mein nahi hoti. Ye Peer-to-Peer network par kaam karti hai, jiska matlab hai transaction seedha ek insaan se dusre tak pahunchta hai.

Blockchain Technology

Ye ek digital ledger (khata) hai jahan har transaction record hota hai. Isse kabhi mitaya ya badla nahi ja sakta, jo isse 100% transparent banata hai.

Cryptography

Advanced math coding ka use karke transactions ko secure kiya jata hai. Isliye iska naam 'Crypto' pada hai kyuki ise hack karna lagbhag namumkin hai.

3. Mining vs Staking

Proof of Work (Mining)

Bitcoin jaise coins computers ki power use karke naye coins banate hain. Is process ko mining kehte hain, jisme bohot electricity kharch hoti hai.

Proof of Stake (Staking)

Ethereum jaise coins mein aap apne coins ko network mein lock karke interest earn karte hain. Ye mining se zyada eco-friendly hai.

Market Cap

Total coins multiplied by current price. Ye batata hai ki kisi crypto project ki market mein kitni badi value hai.

4. Crypto Wallet & Security

Hot Wallets

Ye internet se connected hote hain (Jaise Trust Wallet ya Binance). Ise daily trading ke liye use kiya jata hai lekin ye thode kam secure hote hain.

Cold Wallets (Hardware)

Ye ek Pendrive ki tarah dikhte hain aur offline hote hain. Ye sabse zyada safe hote hain kyuki inka internet se koi lena-dena nahi hota.

Private Key

Ye aapka password hai. Agar aapne ise kho diya, toh aapke paise hamesha ke liye gayab ho jayenge. Isliye ise kabhi kisi ko na batayein.

5. Crypto Terminology (Shabdawali)

HODL: Long term ke liye crypto ko hold karke rakhna.

FOMO: Fear of Missing Out—Jab log darr ke maare upar jaati market mein paisa lagate hain.

FUD: Market mein galat afwah failana taaki prices niche girein.

Bull Market: Jab prices lagatar upar ja rahe hon.

Bear Market: Jab market crash ho rahi ho aur prices niche ja rahe hon.
